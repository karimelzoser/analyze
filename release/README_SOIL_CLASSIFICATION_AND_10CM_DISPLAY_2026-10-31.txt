Engineering preview; project owner presentation requirements

WHAT CHANGED (DISPLAY AND REPORTING ONLY)
- Soil-behavior labels use the project's supplied Ic chart:
  Ic <1.31: gravelly / dense sand
  1.31 <= Ic <1.59: clean sand
  1.59 <= Ic <1.83: sand with silt
  1.83 <= Ic <2.276: silty sand
  2.276 <= Ic <2.50: silty sand to sandy silt
  2.50 <= Ic <2.68: sandy silt to silty sand
  2.68 <= Ic <2.95: silt mixture (clayey silt to silty clay)
  2.95 <= Ic <3.10: silty clay
  3.10 <= Ic <3.60: clay
  Ic >=3.60: organic soils / peat-like.
- If the source has missing/invalid Ic after a silt interval, the missing
  material is UNKNOWN, NOT CLAY. Unknown is not itself a CPT computation error.
- The new Reportable Silt Layers Excel worksheet lists ALL independent
  observed Ic>=2.60 intervals with thickness STRICTLY greater than 0.10 m.
  Multiple qualifying depths from one physical CPT are listed separately;
  sand and unknown gaps are never bridged.
- The native PDF and appended official-source PDF carry a dedicated page
  of every reportable screened silt interval.
- The interactive atlas SILT ONLY view shows only measured individual
  intervals >0.10m; the full layered SBT view remains accessible.
- Layers <=0.10m do not appear as accepted silt in main reports or maps
  and ARE NOT presented as zero-thickness material. They remain in expert
  source/measurement audit evidence where appropriate.
- Repeated physical files for the same nominal CPT retain independent
  output and source naming.
- All numerical CPT calculations, Robertson Ic equation, groundwater,
  instrument coverage checks, smoothing rules and original project-specific
  0.05m internal persistence screening are unchanged.

ENGINEERING LIMITATION
CPT Ic describes inferred soil-behavior type (SBT), not a laboratory USCS
classification. Unknown data cannot prove either clay or clean sand.
An operator-selected display threshold is not an analytical proof that
thin material is absent from the ground. Geotechnical engineering review
is required for source-retentests and foundation design acceptance.
